Record food portions with CherrySunny and see your nutrition totals by day and week. Choose a product from the catalog, enter an amount, select a meal and date, and preview the nutrition before saving.

The Day view groups entries by meal and shows totals for energy, protein, fat, carbohydrates, fiber, sugars, and salt. The Week view offers a seven-day chart, counts of logged days and portions, and weekly nutrition totals. Shortcuts make it easy to log recently used products again.

The catalog includes six starter products and labels previously opened and stored on your device. Search online by name, brand, or barcode, or scan a barcode on a supported device. You can also open the product's page on Open Food Facts.

See today's summary in an iPhone widget, and view the summary and recent entries on a paired Apple Watch. The app interface is in English.
